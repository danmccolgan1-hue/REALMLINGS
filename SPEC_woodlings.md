# Woodlings: the user's spec (as given), with the plan

Realmlings Newest Race: Woodlings

I'm creating the third race for "Realmlings", the game, the race is going to be called Woodlings. 
The intention is for them to look tall and graceful. Their faces are long and jaws are pointy. They have pointy ears. They have purple eyes. Their skintone range is from is olive to purple. 
Besides the changes listed below all buildings will need to models to suit them. Make them tall with natural arches drawing on inspiration from Tolkien's elves.
Make the civilians houses look like trees with doors.
Make the civilians have the noticable ears and the olive to purple skin tones.
Add a button to allow the player to select playing as a Woodling.
Add the ability to make a computer player a Woodling.
Their starting province should be a Forest.
Make the capital look like a blend of a grand gothic church built out of a Great Oak Tree, use the Borgove Stave Church as your inspiration. With branches and canopy growing out of it. 
Make the outposts look like moss covered church, use the Borgove Stave Church as your inspiration.
Make the forts have more branches and leaves growing out of the churchlike structure.

Buildings:
Farm			- Same - model should contain apple trees.
Lumber Mill 	- Relatively the same. Different Model
Gold Mine		- Relatively the same. Different Model
Barracks		- Trains Protectors and Champions. (Champions are fundamentally different from Cav and Ogres, see details below)
Stable			- Becomes Hall of Champions, a longhouse inspired building that allows barracks to train Champions.
Marketplace		- Bazaar, looks different but functionally idential to the marketplace. Trade Routes is substitued for something else.
Granary			- Same, different Model
Storehouse		- Same, different Model - Make it look like it was built out of a hollow giant fallen tree 
Bank			- Same, different Model 
Forge			- Same, different Model
Mage Tower		- "Grove" becomes "Majestic Grove" becomes "Magnificent Grove" A tree with a large canopy that gets larger as it is upgraded. 
Temple			- Change to Shrine, a statue of a vailed woman praying humbly
Roads			- Not available to Woodlings

Woodland Bonuses - 20% increased armor in Forest, 10% increased speed in Forest.

Wayfinder		- Becomes Scout. Can Survey and later Stealth. Can pass through borders with trees.
Mason			- Becomes Architect.
Swordsman		- Swords are unavailable to Woodlings, they use Thrusting Swords instead, thinner than swords but functionally identical, only their Tier 4 is still one handed. (Dirk(starting), Gladius, Rapier, Estoc) Rename this unit named Sword Protector.
Spearman		- Works same as Realmlings only renamed to Spear Protector and a new model
Clubman			- Works same as Realmlings only renamed to Club Protector and a new model
Rider			- Becomes Champion, A tall armored warrior, not mounted, with a great helm with antlers that covers the face, only the eyes can be seen. different kinds of Champion Weapons available, Champions move slightly slower than Protectors but hit much harder and have much more health and armor. Champion weapons are two handed weapons that deal 3 times as much as a one handed weapon. They cannot equip shields. They can only wear Plate (Now Ornate) Armor. 
				- Champion Sword (Starting Greatsword)
				- Ancient Hammer (Two Handed Mace)
				- Wrathful Spear (Two Handed Spear)
				- Worldbreaker Axe (Two Handed Axe)
				- Oblivion Blade (Two Handed Greatsword)
				Each weapontype bonuses still applies, but they also should include different speed and health bonuses too.
Mage			- Becomes Druid, Shockwave is kept the same. A Woodling in a green robe.

Shields unavailable
Two-handing available, when Twohanding every other turn the unit does 0 damage, but then when it does damage it does 125% damage.
This applies to Champions too. Champions can twohand without the researched technology, Protectors can't.

Armor
Leather Armor 	- Becomes Spiritweave Armor (Light Gray)
Chain Armor		- Becomes Mithril Armor (Light Teal)
Plate Armor		- Becomes Ornate Armor (Light Purple)
Overall Armor should be identical to the Realmlings.

Herbology		- Unchanged
Necromancy		- Not Available
Arcane			- Unchanged

New Magic Tree	- Elemental
				- Rock Armor - Give the army a shield of 10% the amount of hp in the army. After that shield has been destroyed the rest of the army takes damage like normal.
				- Whispering Rocks - limit 3 per Druid. Can be made on any Mountainous border of a province the Druid is in. The border gives permanent vision to the player for both provinces. So if a mountain border straddles two provinces, the druid can use this spell on the border and get vision in both permanently. 
				- Grand Elemental - A new temporary unit that looks like a towering man made of water. He lasts for 2 minutes and does as much damage as a fully upgraded champion with twice the health. He has 0 armor. Can join armies and capture provinces. 
				- Forest Spirit - limit 1 per alive Druid, placed on a line of trees, amplifies the output of resources of bordering provinces by 25%
				- Town Portal - cooldown of 5 minutes per Druid. 5 second cast teleports the Druid and his attached army to the capital.
